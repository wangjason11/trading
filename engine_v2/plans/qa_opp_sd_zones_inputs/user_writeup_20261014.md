The change we just did in the previous session gives good context for what we are about to do. To rehash, proximity triggers currently happen with reference zones from 2 opposite sides of the parent structure & cycle: sd & opp_sd. While the sd side includes both POI and BOS KL zones, the opp_sd only has CTS zone since POI zones are always created in the same direction as the MS (buy when +1). What we will now do is update the opp_sd side by adding additional zones to reference. Therefore, going forward CTS zone proximity triggers will become opp_sd zones proximity triggers to mirror sd zones proximity triggers in terms of naming.

Here's what I want to do. Currently, on the main structure (1H), we have a sd side (POI) proximity trigger 748 happening on sid_0 cycle_1's pullback wave as the price retraces. This in turn would trigger the first counter sub trigger. Afterwards, the price then rebounds after the pullback, but never gets close enough to CTS zone to trigger a CTS zone proximity trigger. Now the 2 proximity triggers I noted in the previous session (820 and 871) happen on sid_1, so retroactively after the real time has already passed. The trigger markers still exist, but the zones that trigger them are not shown on the chart since they are collapsed and we chose to explicitly only show zones that were active. Nevertheless, those triggers firing are still the correct behavior.

My preference would be that we actually have additional triggers fire around those idx (820 and 871) during the actual live real time on sid_0 cycle_1. The way I propose to do that is to leverage the additional zones created by sub_3, the sub that is running counter to sid_0 sd at those idxes. Similar to how when price in the original main struct retraces toward the sd direction zones (POI & BOS), when the price rebounds after the retrace, there are opposite sd zones available from the most recent sub & sub cycle that's counter to the parent's direction. Now in addition to the original parent CTS zone (sell), we have additional BOS & POI zones (all sells) from the opposite sd sub that we can reference. Here note that although the parent reference is CTS and the sub references are BOS side, the zone directions (buy/sell) will all be the same for the parent & the sub because the sub is counter to the parent. That is an important note: sd zones should all be same direction just like all opp_sd zones should be the opposite direction. Now similar to sd side proximity trigger, amongst all zones available, we choose the one that is closest to the other side as the reference for the proximity trigger. Please double check the above and let me know if I'm mistaken anywhere.

To recap:
SD zones proximity trigger
Reference zones: Parent BOS zone & parent POI zone
Triggers when price reaches closest of the 2 within xx pips during the pullback

Opposite SD zones proximity trigger
Reference zones: Parent CTS & most recent counter sd sub & cycle's BOS zone & POI zone
Triggers when price reaches closest of the 3 within xx pips during the rebound after the pullback. This rebound would eventually form the new cycle wave if it breaks the parent CTS

Now although with the new opposite sd zone proximity trigger we are mixing main & sub structure references, the proximity trigger should still be happening on the main (1H) structure, like all other proximity triggers do. The sub zones simply become an "overlay" reference using the zone's inner price. How the overall process of the triggers happening should still remain the same, we are just changing a reference as if we are changing a parameter.

So my expectation would be we would have one of these opposite sd zones proximity triggers on sid_0 around the same place as idx 820, which is currently a sd zone proximity trigger on sid_1. The downstream impact of having this trigger is widespread:

1. This trigger should now be tied to the triggering of a subsequent confluence sub, which would lead to the creation of a new unique sub that did not exist previously. For downstream processes that previously referenced CTS zone proximity triggers, they will now all reference the broader opposite sd zones proximity triggers instead.

With our existing proximity trigger logic, each next proximity trigger must be on the opposite side of the previous one. Therefore, when the previous CTS side proximity trigger did not exist before this change we are making, the 2nd BOS side proximity trigger never happens. But now, as it re-approaches the parent buy POI zone again, it would trigger a new proximity. I would anticipate this one to be around 830-833.
