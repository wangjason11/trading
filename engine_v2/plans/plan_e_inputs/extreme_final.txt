### summary

**Where "extreme" would still be used.** "Extreme" survives only in names that describe how a candle was found or what it looks like: window and retracement searches, running extremes, "is this a new extreme" checks, price-mapping (finding the lower-timeframe candle that holds a parent candle's high or low), and candle anatomy. The two frozen BOS source labels `initial_prior_extreme` and `pullback_extreme` also stay, because they honestly name a search. There is one new use in the pattern realm: `pattern_extreme_idx` / `pattern_extreme_price`, the highest high or lowest low from the pattern's first candle through its apply candle. That candle becomes `cts_anchor_idx` at exactly one place, where market structure adopts it as the CTS (market_structure.py:1462). Every other current use of "extreme" means a BOS or CTS endpoint and turns into "anchor". Two uses of "ext" mean extension (`ext_end_idx` and the `"EXT"` point tag) and get spelled out.
