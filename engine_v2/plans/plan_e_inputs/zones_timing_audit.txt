[13] GLOSSARY definition. **CTS_ESTABLISHED.idx (the CTS extreme)**: the candle holding the new cycle's CTS price. It is the FIRST argmax of `h` (sd=+1) or argmin of `l` (sd=−1) over the winning breakout pattern's span `[start_idx, max(end_idx, confirmation_idx)]` (`market_structure._cts_from_breakout_event`), and `ev.price` is that high/low. It is a historical (element-definition) field with `anchor_idx ≤ idx ≤ confirmed_at ≤ anchor_idx + range_max_k (5)`. It precedes the moment whenever an earlier pattern candle or a candle skipped by the confirmation search holds the extreme (reference window: M15 1223 vs 1224, 2828 vs 2829). Nothing is emitted when that candle closes, so it locates the CTS price (CTS dot, ad-hoc CTS zone, fib anchor) and is never a lifecycle/timing gate.
    evidence: market_structure.py:1462, :1485-1487, :1658-1686; live 31/31 price==extreme at idx.
    verified_how: live_repro
    confidence: high

[14] GLOSSARY definition. **CTS_ESTABLISHED.meta["confirmed_at"] (the CTS-established moment)**: the apply candle of the breakout pattern that opened the cycle (`_apply_idx`: `end_idx` for SUCCESS, `confirmation_idx` for CONFIRMED).
